01 / The organization

Useful to Passione.
Never quite insiders.

La Squadra Esecuzioni is Passione's hitman team. Their assignments put them among the organization's most capable Stand users, while their compensation and rank leave them dissatisfied.

The team turns against Passione's leadership after their demands for a greater share go unanswered. The records below summarize their known history, affiliations, Stand abilities, and contract terms.
